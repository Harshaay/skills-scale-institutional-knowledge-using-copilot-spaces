# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers are responsible for the usability and user experience of products and features. They work to ensure solutions are accessible and intuitive.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces and flows
- Conduct usability testing and synthesize findings
- Create prototypes and design assets
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Developers for implementation

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and user-centered designs
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and walkthroughs
- User testing reports
- Figma files or design specs shared with the team

### Interactions
- Partners with Product Managers early in requirements gathering
- Works with Developers during handoff and implementation
- Reviews features post-release to validate user experience

---

## Technical Writers

### Role Summary
Technical Writers produce clear, user-focused documentation for both internal and external audiences.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user guides, release notes, and internal process docs
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of documentation
- Gather information from subject matter experts

### Goals
- Improve user enablement and reduce support requests
- Keep documentation up to date with ongoing product changes

### Typical Communication
- Draft reviews with Developers and PMs
- Release documentation summaries
- Internal doc updates for new or changed processes

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers to capture technical details
- Partners with Product Managers for product context and release info
- Supports QA with testing procedures and documentation

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the test strategy and coordinate quality assurance activities across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the overall test approach
- Organize and execute test plans, including manual and automated testing
- Ensure all acceptance criteria are validated before release

### Goals
- Maintain a high standard of product quality and reliability
- Identify issues early and ensure timely resolution

### Typical Communication
- Test plan documents and bug reports
- Status updates in standups and delivery reviews
- Escalations for blocking defects

### Interactions
- Works with Developers to understand implementation and write effective tests
- Partners with PMs and Product Managers to ensure requirements are testable
- Participates in release sign-offs with the team

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers maintain the automation, reliability, and scalability of build, test, and deployment systems.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor deployments and system health
- Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure issues

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Ensure system uptime and quick incident response

### Typical Communication
- Infra status dashboards and alerts
- Deployment runbooks
- Incident reports and postmortems

### Interactions
- Supports Developers and QA with build/test environments
- Coordinates with Project Managers for release planning
- Collaborates with all roles on incident response and reliability improvements
